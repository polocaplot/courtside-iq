COURTSIDE IQ — REALISM ENGINE UPDATE (2026-10-24)

WHAT CHANGED
1. On-court events now use per-minute player rates. Previous shooting,
   passing, rebound and defensive event weights multiplied by the player's
   projected minutes a second time, systematically over-favoring starters.
   Small-minute samples are shrunk with conservative exposure priors.
2. The lineup scheduler now compares each player's actual played slots to
   his game-specific target, prioritizes remaining minute deficits, keeps
   some lineup continuity, and honors the caller's foul/injury eligibility.
3. In possession mode, overtime is simulated with extra shot, foul, assist,
   rebound and turnover events, rather than inventing points after regulation.
   Overtime is approximated with 5-minute event periods. Foul state currently
   resets at overtime boundaries, which is a remaining realism limitation.
4. Late-game trailing teams modestly increase 3-point shot selection;
   leading teams modestly reduce it. Threes are more likely assisted than twos.
5. The diagnostic includes realism_audit: scoring identity, minute
   conservation, overtime incidence, and player minute deviations from priors.
   Large (>3 percentage point) calibration shifts are explicitly flagged.

TESTING
112 automated tests pass, including new minute-budget and overtime
accounting tests. Tests verify structural correctness only, NOT forecasting
accuracy. This update has not been run against live NBA.com on the user's Mac.

STILL NEEDED FOR VERIFIED PREDICTIVE REALISM
- Historical walk-forward validation of the FULL possession engine, with
  authentic pregame injuries, roster and lineup data for each historical date.
- Coaching substitution timing and observed lineup frequencies, not just
  approximate five-player rotation intervals.
- Real matchup-level defensive tracking (not available from aggregate feeds).
- Empirical calibration of player shot, foul, and usage models against held-out
  actual game distributions; do not confuse matching PPG with prediction skill.
- Genuine trained ML verification for an upcoming game.
