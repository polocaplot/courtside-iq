COURTSIDE IQ — Quarter-by-quarter simulation update

Detailed possession mode now records player and team events at each game's
own four quarter boundaries. Quarter scores, quarter-ending cumulative scores,
team box stats, and individual quarter box stats are all aggregated from the
same simulation outcomes as the final game projections. A selected quarter's
player averages can be exported as CSV. Overtime contributions are shown
separately; they are not redistributed into Q4.

The quarter split uses each game's possession pace, not exact shot clocks or
verified NBA play-by-play. Quarter predictions and the team scoring calibration
are NOT historically backtested. The simulator still independently calibrates
both teams to their own pregame offensive/defensive targets and reports large
corrections. Player minute priors use available current/recent evidence but do
not establish a coach's confirmed rotation or injury-return schedule.

Validation: test_quarter_statistics.py verifies quarter score reconciliation,
player stat consistency, minutes, overtime separation, and baseline honesty.
